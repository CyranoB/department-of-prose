# Pattern 11: Rule of three

Catalogue version **1.0.0**. [Full entry](../catalogue.md).

## What to assess

Repeated triplets add empty third items; preserve genuine three-part inventories and rhetorical triplets that fit the genre.

## Evidence and limits

The source describes a current, context-dependent AI-style association. It does not identify the author of a passage.

**Source and evidence type:** [WikiProject AI Cleanup, “Rule of three”](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing&oldid=1376815715#Rule_of_three) — field observation.

**Evidence type:** observation. **AI-association support:** limited.

**Strength and scope:** Support concerns repetitive use or needless third items, not any three-part list. Triplets are an established human rhetorical device. **Reviewed:** 2026-09-27.

## Editorial use

An editorial repair can be useful when the exact passage shows the problem and the false-positive guard has been checked.
